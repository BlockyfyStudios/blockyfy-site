Fulfillment & cancellation

How digital membership benefits are delivered, corrected and ended.

Effective: August 9, 2026

1. Digital delivery only

Blockyfy memberships provide digital benefits. Nothing is physically shipped, there are no shipping charges, and no delivery address is required for membership fulfillment.

The exact benefits are the ones listed for the selected plan. Builds and beta invitations are delivered only for plans that explicitly include them and only when a corresponding build or test is available.

2. Discord verification and delivery

To receive Discord benefits:

  1. Join the Blockyfy Discord server.
  2. At Stripe Checkout, provide the actual Discord username for that account, not its display name.
  3. Allow direct messages from server members so the Blockyfy bot can contact you.
  4. Open the one-time verification link sent by the bot.
  5. After Stripe payment and Discord verification are confirmed, the system assigns the role associated with the active plan.

Verification is not complete merely because payment succeeded. The customer must be in the server, be found under the supplied username, receive the direct message and use the one-time link.

3. Minecraft information

The Minecraft username field at checkout is optional. If you provide it for an applicable test build, enter it exactly. Where Minecraft authentication is enabled, the service may verify the corresponding profile and UUID before granting access. A Minecraft username does not replace subscription or Discord verification.

4. Delivery problems and corrections

If you entered an incorrect Discord or Minecraft username, did not receive the bot message, or received the wrong role, contact contact@blockyfy.net from the checkout email. Include the plan and the corrected username, but do not send a password, full card number or one-time verification link.

Blockyfy can review and correct membership account fields. Temporary delays can occur while Stripe, Discord or other required services are unavailable.

5. How to cancel or change a plan

Open the Stripe customer portal and enter the same email used at checkout. Follow the secure sign-in link Stripe sends to that email, then use the available controls to cancel or change the membership.

A scheduled cancellation stops future renewals. Unless Stripe Checkout or the portal states otherwise, current benefits continue until the end of the already-paid billing period and then end. A plan change and any prorated amount are shown by Stripe before confirmation.

6. Failed, reversed or ended payments

Benefits may remain pending or be removed if a payment is not confirmed, a subscription expires, or a charge is reversed. If you believe access ended incorrectly, contact support with the checkout email and Stripe receipt or invoice reference.

7. Refunds and support

Cancellation does not automatically refund a completed charge. Review the Refund policy for eligibility and request instructions. Fulfillment and cancellation questions can be sent to contact@blockyfy.net.
