§ 01

What it is

Built like a game, not a mod pack

Flagship · In development

A full game, grown inside Minecraft

This is not a texture pack with powers. You can live as a fighter or build an empire inside the same persistent galaxy. Inhabited worlds have leaders, economies, politics and conflicts; borders are backed by soldiers, not menu flags. Combat and conquest are resolved by the server, so a modified client cannot invent power or territory.

Development happens in the open. Systems are engineered, tested in game with the community and shipped when they hold up, never before. Save compatibility is treated as a contract: a world you started months ago still opens.

The real fantasy

Do not just visit the galaxy. Become one of its powers.

Every inhabited world can become an ally, a responsibility or a target. Build something large enough and your organization becomes part of the same history as the powers that were already there.

01 / A galaxy already alive

A world with rulers, needs and grudges

Civilizations are societies, not scenery. Their people, economy and politics continue whether you help, exploit or ignore them.

  • Living civilizationsGenerated species have leaders, political blocs, markets, shortages, technological eras, conflicts and changing relationships.
  • Known galactic powersThe Cold Empire, Saiyan Army and Red Ribbon Army rise, expand or fall with the calendar; the Galactic Patrol acts across that same map.
  • History with consequencesOccupations, rebellions, liberation, destruction and restoration remain part of that world's story.

02 / Your empire

Put your own banner on the map

Found an organization with ranks, soldiers, a shared treasury and territory that has to be defended physically.

  • Claim and govern planetsBuild garrisons, collect tribute from occupied civilizations and keep an army paid across multiple worlds.
  • Raid, protect or liberateOrder assaults, coordinate allies, defend protectorates and restore a civilization without annexing it.
  • Change borders permanentlyNegotiate treaties, vassalage or consensual annexation; seize worlds, survive uprisings and destroy or restore eligible planets.

Development note: the empire experience is under active multiplayer and performance verification. Scope, balance and availability may change before the public alpha.

§ 04

Questions

Answered straight

When does the mod release?

When it is ready. We do not announce dates, because we only ship systems that hold up in real play. Supporters can follow development from the inside; trying builds before public release is limited to plans that explicitly include early access.

Is the mod paid?

No. The public version is free to download and play, always. Supporting funds development. Early builds or closed beta invitations are included only when they are explicitly listed in the selected plan and a build or test is available.

How do I receive my supporter perks?

First join the Blockyfy Discord server. At checkout, enter your actual Discord username, not your display name. The Minecraft username field is optional, but it never unlocks access by itself. Allow direct messages from server members, then open the one-time verification link sent by the Blockyfy bot. Your Discord role and any included early-access build or closed beta remain locked until payment and Discord verification are confirmed. Dev logs, builds and beta invitations are delivered through the community server only when included in your selected plan.

Where can I get support?

The Blockyfy Discord server is our primary support channel for access, account verification, builds, installation and gameplay. For billing, privacy or legal requests, email contact@blockyfy.net.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Sign in to the secure Stripe customer portal with the email used at checkout to cancel or change your tier. You keep your perks until the end of the billing period.

What do I need to play?

Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1 with Forge. Detailed installation instructions ship with every build.

Will an update break my world?

That is the one thing we refuse to let happen. A world you started months ago still opens, with your character and everything you built in it. Any change that touches saved data is versioned on purpose before it ships, and if a change cannot clear that bar it does not go out.
