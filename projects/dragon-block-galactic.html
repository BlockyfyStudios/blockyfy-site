Their worlds

Worlds with rulers, needs and grudges

These are societies, not scenery. They keep going whether you help them, exploit them or ignore them.

  • Living civilizationsEvery species has leaders, political blocs, markets, shortages, a technology era and its own conflicts.
  • Known galactic powersThe Cold Empire, the Saiyan Army and the Red Ribbon Army rise and fall with the calendar, while the Galactic Patrol polices the same space.
  • Consequences that stayOccupations, rebellions and liberations stay part of each world's story. So does a planet you destroy or restore.

Your empire

Put your own banner on the map

Found an organization with ranks, soldiers and a shared treasury, and hold territory you have to defend in person.

  • Claim and govern planetsBuild garrisons, collect tribute from occupied civilizations and keep an army paid across several worlds.
  • Raid, protect or liberateLead assaults with your allies, defend your protectorates or drive out a world's occupiers without taking it over.
  • Redraw the bordersSign treaties, take vassals or welcome a world that wants to join. Seize worlds, survive uprisings, and destroy or restore planets.

Early-access note: empires are still being tested in multiplayer and may change between builds.

Ask on Discord
Can I play it right now?

Yes, if your plan includes early access: Super Warrior, Legendary or the all-access Blockyfy Supporter. The Warrior plan doesn't include the game. New builds go out through our Discord. Expect bugs, balance changes and big new features between builds.

When is the full release?

When it's ready. We don't set dates. We only ship a feature once it holds up in real play. Until then, playing builds is limited to plans that explicitly include early access.

What do I need to play?

Three things: a plan that includes early access, your own official Minecraft Java Edition account and Forge for Minecraft 1.20.1. Every build comes with installation instructions. If you get stuck, ask on Discord.

Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes. Play together on a server running the mod. Each player needs their own plan that includes early access, and everyone must be on the same build as the server.

How do I receive my supporter perks?

Subscribe, then join our Discord and open the one-time verification link our bot sends you. At checkout, enter your actual Discord username, not your display name, and your exact Minecraft username. Both fields are required, but a Minecraft username never unlocks access by itself. Your Discord role and any included build or beta unlock once payment and Discord verification are confirmed. Your first protected Minecraft login then links your game account automatically.

Where can I get support?

The Blockyfy Discord server is our primary support channel for access, verification, builds, installation and gameplay. For billing, privacy or legal requests, email contact@blockyfy.net.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. To cancel or change your plan, sign in to the secure Stripe customer portal with the email you used at checkout. You keep your perks until the end of the billing period.
