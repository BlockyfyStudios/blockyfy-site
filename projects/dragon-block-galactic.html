§ 01

What it is

Built like a game, not a mod pack

Flagship · In development

A full game, grown inside Minecraft

This is not a texture pack with powers. You create a warrior, train under masters, cross a procedural galaxy and leave real scars on real planets. Every system is resolved on the server, so fights stay fair on any server and a modified client cannot invent power.

Development happens in the open. Systems are engineered, tested in game with the community and shipped when they hold up, never before. Save compatibility is treated as a contract: a world you started months ago still opens.

§ 04

Questions

Answered straight

When does the mod release?

When it is ready. We do not announce dates, because we only ship systems that hold up in real play. Supporters follow progress from the inside and try builds first.

Is the mod paid?

No. The public version is free to download and play, always. Supporting funds development and unlocks perks like early access builds and closed betas.

How do I receive my supporter perks?

You enter your Minecraft nick and your Discord username during checkout. Your supporter role on Discord and your beta access are granted automatically, and dev logs and early builds are delivered through the community server.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed through a secure Stripe customer portal where you can cancel or change your tier in one click. You keep your perks until the end of the billing period.

What do I need to play?

Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1 with Forge. Detailed installation instructions ship with every build.

Will an update break my world?

That is the one thing we refuse to let happen. A world you started months ago still opens, with your character and everything you built in it. Any change that touches saved data is versioned on purpose before it ships, and if a change cannot clear that bar it does not go out.
