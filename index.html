§ 02

How we build

Rules, not slogans

01

Your world never breaks

An update is not allowed to ruin a save. A world you started months ago still opens, with your character and everything you built in it. Anything that touches stored data is versioned on purpose before it ships, and nothing ships if it cannot pass that bar.

02

The server judges the fight

Your game sends what you meant to do and nothing else. Damage, power and world state are decided on the server, so a modified client cannot invent strength and a fight stays fair on any server you join.

03

Player funded

No publisher, no ads, no loot boxes. Supporters fund the work, see builds first and vote on what comes next. The mods stay free either way, which is the whole point of doing it this way.
