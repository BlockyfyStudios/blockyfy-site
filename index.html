Support the studio

Every public release is free to play. One membership backs the whole studio and includes the benefits listed in its plan, such as early builds, closed betas and studio credits. Nothing you pay for touches game balance.

One membership for the whole studio. Every current and future project is included. Discord perks require joining our server and completing one-time verification. Subscriptions are not open yet.

To receive Discord perks, join the Blockyfy server, use your actual Discord username (not your display name), allow direct messages from server members, and open the one-time verification link sent by the bot. Build and beta access is provided only when the selected plan explicitly includes it and a build or test is available.

Prices are recurring monthly charges in USD. Before subscribing, read our Membership terms, Refund policy, Fulfillment and cancellation policy, and Privacy policy.
