How to get in

  1. Subscribe

    At checkout, enter your actual Discord username (not your display name) and your exact Minecraft username.

  2. Join Discord and verify

    Join the Blockyfy server, allow direct messages from server members, and open the one-time verification link our bot sends you.

    Discord membership is required for protected access. Until you verify, early-access builds and closed betas remain locked even if a Minecraft username was provided at checkout.

  3. Download and play

    Grab the latest build from Discord. On your first protected Minecraft login, Blockyfy verifies your official game session with Mojang and links the proven account automatically. No second approval needed.

Need help? The Blockyfy Discord server is our primary support channel for access, verification, builds, installation and gameplay. Use contact@blockyfy.net for billing, privacy or legal requests.

Listed prices represent recurring monthly charges in USD. Stripe checkout may instead present a supported local currency, which becomes the subscription's billing currency. Each plan includes only the benefits listed on it, and a build or test must be available for a build benefit to apply.
